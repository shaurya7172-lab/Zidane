About Zidane

Zinedine Zidane, popularly known as "Zizou," is a French football legend. Born on June 23, 1972, in Marseille, France, he is widely regarded as one of the greatest players in the history of the game. He was famous for his elegance, vision, ball control, and incredible technique.

Career Journey

Zidane played as an attacking midfielder for world-class clubs and the France national team:

  • Cannes & Bordeaux: Where he started his professional journey in France.
  • Juventus: Won two Serie A titles and reached two Champions League finals.
  • Real Madrid: Scored one of the greatest volleys ever in the 2002 Champions League final.
  • France National Team: Led France to victory in the 1998 World Cup and Euro 2000.

Major Achievements

Zidane won almost every trophy possible, both as a player and a manager:

  • FIFA World Cup Winner (1998)
  • UEFA European Championship Winner (2000)
  • Ballon d'Or Winner (1998)
  • 3x FIFA World Player of the Year
  • 3x UEFA Champions League Winner as Real Madrid Manager (Consecutive)
